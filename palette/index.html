Palette check

Paste your colors. See every text and background pair that fails WCAG AA, by how much, and the nearest shade that passes. Nothing leaves your browser.

Your colors

Hex, rgb, hsl, oklch, named colors, or CSS variables pasted straight from a stylesheet. Up to 24 colors.

Every pair

Contrast ratio of each text color (rows) on each background color (columns)

Rows are the text color, columns are the background. AA for body text needs 4.5 to 1; large text (about 24px, or 19px bold) and interface outlines need 3 to 1; AAA for body text needs 7 to 1. Ratios follow WCAG 2.1. Colors with transparency are checked as if opaque.

Failing pairs, and the nearest shade that passes

    Each fix keeps the hue and moves only the lightness of one color until the pair reaches 4.5 to 1. It is the smallest honest change, not a redesign. The swatches show the pair as it is, then with each fix.

    Fix the whole system

    A patched color is a start. A finished palette has every tone generated to a contrast target, a type scale, and a check in the build so the pairs cannot regress. That is part of every Helical project. Send this palette with your brief and you have a scope and a price by the next day.
